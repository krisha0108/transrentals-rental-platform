📋 Introduction

What is the Vendor Platform?

The TransRentals Vendor Platform is a full-featured B2B dashboard for transport vendors. Vendors can register, get verified, list their services and fleet, respond to customer quote requests, manage direct bookings, track staff, and monitor their financials — all from a single dashboard.

✅ Multi-service support (Bike, Car, Bus, Truck, Luxury, Equipment)
✅ Web dashboard + Mobile app (JWT-based)
✅ Real-time chat with customers
✅ Fleet tracking with docs, maintenance, fuel
✅ Staff management with attendance & payroll
✅ P&L tracking per booking with expense breakdown

📲 Operations

Direct Bookings

Vendor-initiated bookings for walk-in, phone, or WhatsApp customers

Two Ways to Create

📝 Manual Entry
Vendor adds booking directly from dashboard at /vendor/direct-bookings/add. Fill customer info, service, dates, amount.
🔗 Shared Booking Link
Vendor creates a unique URL at /vendor/booking-links. Customer fills the form → booking created automatically.
