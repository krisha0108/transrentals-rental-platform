Slide 03

3 Root Causes

These three problems explain 95% of the conversion loss.

#1
Chat-first kills first-time visitors

Bus and truck use a chatbot (Shivi) as the primary CTA. Users from Google are strangers — they want a form, not a conversation. Chatbots have 60–70% higher abandonment than forms for cold traffic.

Proof: Bike/self-drive have zero chatbot → highest inquiry counts.

#2
No price anchor = no trust = bounce

Luxury car and car rental show zero pricing information on the hero. Users assume it's expensive or spam. Without a starting price like "Fortuner from ₹4,500" they can't self-qualify.

Bike pages show ₹399/day → instant self-qualification → more bookings.

#3
Quote flow = long wait = lost lead

Once a user submits a quote on bus/luxury/truck, they wait for a callback. If no one calls within 10–15 minutes, they're already on a competitor site. There's no WhatsApp auto-acknowledgment confirming receipt.

Fix: Instant WhatsApp message on form submit buys goodwill time.
