## 🎯 Keyword Strategy

### Target Keywords Per Page Type

#### Service × Location Pages
- Primary: "{service} {location}" (e.g., "bike on rent goa")
- Secondary: "{service} in {location}" (e.g., "bike rental in goa")
- Long-tail: "rent {service} {location}", "{service} hire {location}"
- Questions: "how to rent {service} in {location}"

**Example for Goa:**
- bike on rent goa
- bike rental goa
- bike hire goa
- rent bike goa
- two wheeler on rent goa
- scooty rental goa
- royal enfield goa
- activa on rent goa

#### Fleet × Location Pages
- Primary: "{vehicle} on rent in {location}"
- Secondary: "{vehicle} rental {location}"
- Brand: "{brand} rental {location}"
- Long-tail: "rent {vehicle} {location}", "{vehicle} hire {location}"

**Example for Royal Enfield Classic 350 in Goa:**
- royal enfield classic 350 on rent in goa
- classic 350 rental goa
- re classic 350 hire goa
- rent royal enfield goa
- royal enfield goa price
- classic 350 goa booking
